Privacy Policy

Focus D · Last updated June 20, 2026

Our Stance on Privacy

Focus D does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything you do in the app stays on your device, period.

Data Collection

Focus D collects zero data. We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Crash logs or diagnostics (unless you opt in via Apple's system-level setting)

Data Storage

All app data — your tasks, focus sessions, wishlist items, tomato balance, profile settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in data storage. We have no access to it.

Third-Party Services

Focus D does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only external services are Apple's system frameworks (Reminders, Calendar, StoreKit for subscriptions), which are governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Focus D is rated 4+ and does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to collect.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date will be revised. Since we don't collect data, there's not much to change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out at da5h.studio@outlook.com.

隐私政策（中文）

我们对隐私的立场

Focus D 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。 没有账号、没有服务器、没有分析统计。 你在应用中做的一切，都只存在于你的设备上。

数据收集

Focus D 收集的数据量为零。我们不收集：

  • 个人信息（姓名、邮箱、位置等）
  • 使用分析或行为追踪
  • 设备标识符或指纹
  • 崩溃日志或诊断数据（除非你通过 Apple 系统级设置选择共享）

数据存储

所有应用数据——任务、专注会话、心愿清单、番茄余额、 个人资料和偏好设置——均仅存储在你的设备本地。 我们无法访问这些数据。

第三方服务

Focus D 不集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。 唯一涉及的外部服务是 Apple 系统框架（提醒事项、日历、StoreKit 订阅）， 这些由 Apple 自身的隐私政策约束。

儿童隐私

Focus D 评级为 4+，不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。 因为没有东西可以收集。

政策变更

如本隐私政策发生变更，更新版本将发布在此页面，并更新"最后更新"日期。 由于我们不收集数据，实际上没什么可改的。
